# Halcyon Freight — Warehouse Operations Manual

This manual applies to every Halcyon Freight distribution centre (DC). Site sections list the local details; the general sections apply everywhere unless a site section says otherwise.

## Site DC-01 — Rotterdam

### Site contacts (Rotterdam)

The site lead for DC-01 is Lotte Brink. Out-of-hours escalations go to the regional duty manager.

### Layout (Rotterdam)

DC-01 operates 8 loading docks and has racking for 14,000 pallet positions. Docks are numbered from the west wall. Hazardous goods are stored in the caged area only.

### Shifts (Rotterdam)

The early shift at DC-01 starts at 06:00. Shift handover takes place at the team board and must cover open exceptions, damaged stock and equipment faults.

### Cold chain (Rotterdam)

Chilled storage at DC-01 is held at 2 °C. Temperature probes are read every two hours and logged in the WMS.

### Emergency procedures (Rotterdam)

When the alarm sounds, stop all equipment, leave by the nearest marked exit and do not use the dock doors. The assembly point for DC-01 is the north car park by lamp post 4. Fire wardens carry the attendance list.

## Equipment

### Kestrel sorter

The Kestrel crossbelt sorter handles parcels up to 30 kg. Belts are inspected weekly. The replacement drive belt has part number KS-4471-B; the tensioner kit is KS-0932-T. Never open the induction cover while the sorter is in automatic mode.

### Forklift charging

Charge lead-acid forklift batteries only in the ventilated charging bay. Charging starts when the battery is at 20 % or less; opportunity charging in breaks is not allowed for lead-acid units.

After a full charge the battery must rest before use. The rest period is 45 minutes, so that the electrolyte cools and gas disperses. Lithium-ion units do not need a rest period.

### Stretch wrapper

Pallets leaving the site are wrapped with at least five turns at the base and three at the top. Pre-stretch is set to 250 % for standard film.

## Goods in

### Receiving

Check the seal number against the advance shipping notice before the doors are opened. A broken or missing seal is reported as exception code GI-17 and the load is quarantined until security clears it.

### Refrigerated arrivals

Refrigerated pallets may stay at ambient dock temperature for at most 25 minutes. After that they are returned to chilled storage and a probe reading is taken before putaway continues.

## Returns

Customer returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery. Returned stock is graded A (resell), B (rework) or C (scrap). Grade C items are logged with reason code RT-09 and collected by the recycling partner every Thursday.

## Error codes (WMS)

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| E-1042 | Scanner lost connection | Re-dock the scanner, then log in again |
| E-7731 | Location over capacity | Move the pallet to the overflow lane and raise a slotting ticket |
| E-2208 | Duplicate SSCC label | Quarantine both pallets and call inventory control |
| E-5150 | Lot number expired | Do not pick; move the stock to the QA hold area |
